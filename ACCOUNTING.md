## Bounty — denominated in bits

The bounty for each accepted contribution is **2 bits (2 coins)** — the same fair-exchange unit, earned by the deed (a gate-passing, receipted contribution) and owed by commercial use. Total bounty accounted on the ledger so far: **56,752 bits**. This is an accounting bounty in bits/coins — **integrity, not a cash prize**. Heroes and traitors by deeds, not claims.

**Not tracked here: tokens.** This repo measures coins (2 per receipt) and 64-bit harmony coins; it does not measure tokens, so no token count or token-to-coin rate is shown — measuring an unmeasured quantity would be an assertion without a receipt. Measure, do not assert.
