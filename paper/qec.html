── 8 · THE HONEST BOUND: DECIDED ON A PHYSICAL (CLASSICAL) DEVICE, NOT A QUANTUM FAULT-TOLERANT ONE ────── These counts ARE computed on a physical device during the build — the classical processor running the kernel over a finite range of distances, real arithmetic on real silicon. What that build does NOT instantiate is a physical fault-tolerant QUANTUM device: deciding that a distance-3 surface code has 13 qubits, on a classical CPU, error- corrects no physical qubit. The ISO audit's fault-tolerance score measures error correction on physical qubits, and the build's classical arithmetic supplies none. The device that counts is real; the quantum device it counts is not.
