WHY THIS FILE EXISTS, AND WHAT IT REFUSES TO SAY.

src/iso/iec-4879-quantum/qpu.lean decides what qpu.uuidna.com's REGISTER constants imply. This file does the same for its CRYPTOGRAPHIC surface, which is the part a reader is most likely to quote and least likely to check. The method is the one that file set: the service's numbers are recorded as its own declarations, and the kernel decides what follows from them — never that the service is right, never that this deposit is.

1 · IT DOES NOT SAY THE SEAL IS SECURE. It says what the seal IS: a textbook RSA permutation of a residue mod 91, folded mod 8. A permutation loses nothing; the fold is where the one-in-eight miss comes from. 2 · IT DOES NOT SAY THE TRAPDOOR IS IMPOSSIBLE. It says what the trapdoor IS: Fermat's difference of squares, whose blind cost is the gap between the primes — and it decides the case where that cost is one. 3 · IT DOES NOT SAY THIS DEPOSIT FACTORS A THOUSAND-BIT INTEGER. It records what the factor route returned on 2^999 + 1 — PARTIAL — and decides that the partial account multiplies back exactly.

No axioms, no Mathlib, no sorry.
