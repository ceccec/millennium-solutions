widths.lean ​

src/iso/iec-9834-8-identifiers/widths.lean · namespace Widths · 24 theorems · wing the address

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS — TWO LEADS CROSSING.

scripts/leads.ts reports forty-two theorems that "only read back a hand-set value — a certificate, not a proof", and asks for each to be restated as a law with its inverse. Six of them are in asymmetric.lean, over uuidBytes = 16, publicKeyBytes = 32, signatureBytes = 64.

scripts/coils.ts reports which expressions compute the same thing and may stand for one another.

Crossing them finds something neither reports alone. src/iso/22739-ledger/capacity.lean types container = 128 — the UUID's width IN BITS — and asymmetric.lean types uuidBytes = 16 — the same width IN BYTES. They are ONE FACT in two units, in two files, with no theorem between them. Either could have been edited while the other stayed green, and every gate in this tree would have passed: the constants are internally consistent within each file and there was nothing to be inconsistent WITH.

That is what a coil is for, and it is what the "laws" lead is asking for. The law is the unit conversion, its inverse is the other direction, and both are decided over the domain rather than at the one instance the constants happen to sit at.

AND THE THREE SIZES ARE ONE FAMILY. 16, 32, 64 are 16 × 2^k for k = 0, 1, 2 — the key is two identifiers and the signature is two keys, which theorem 3 decides as a doubling rather than as three literals that happen to be in ratio.

No axioms, no Mathlib, no sorry.

Theorem 150 (the_uuid_width_is_one_fact_in_two_units)sealed.

toBits(uuidBytes)=container∧toBytes(container)=uuidBytes
toBits uuidBytes == container ∧ toBytes container == uuidBytes
LaTeX source
\mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{uuidBytes}\right) = \mathrm{container} \land \mathrm{toBytes}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{container}\right) = \mathrm{uuidBytes}

── 1 · THE TWO FILES STATE ONE WIDTH ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── The binding that did not exist. capacity.lean's container and asymmetric.lean's uuidBytes are the same width, and from here neither can move without this refusing.

Proof. by decide — by evaluation; no domain is walked. □

Theorem 151 (bytes_to_bits_and_back_is_the_identity)sealed.

∀b∈{0,…,39},toBytes(toBits(b))=b∧∀n∈{0,…,39},toBits(toBytes(n))=n=nmod8=0
(List.range 40).all (fun b => toBytes (toBits b) == b) ∧ (List.range 40).all (fun n => (toBits (toBytes n) == n) == (n % 8 == 0))
LaTeX source
\forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \mathrm{toBytes}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right)\right) = b \land \forall n \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{toBytes}\mathopen{}\left(n\right)\right) = n = n \bmod 8 = 0

── 2 · THE CONVERSION IS A LAW WITH ITS INVERSE, OVER THE DOMAIN ───────────────────────────────────────── What the leads report asks for: not the constant read back, but the law it is an instance of, decided at every byte width in range. Bytes to bits and back is the identity; bits to bytes and back is NOT, except on multiples of eight — and saying so is the honest half, because truncation is where a unit conversion silently loses a claim.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 1,600 cases. □

Theorem 152 (the_key_doubles_the_identifier_and_the_signature_doubles_the_key)sealed.

publicKeyBytes == 2 * uuidBytes ∧ signatureBytes == 2 * publicKeyBytes ∧ signatureBytes == 4 * uuidBytes ∧ (List.range 3).all (fun k => [uuidBytes, publicKeyBytes, signatureBytes].getD k 0 == 16 * 2 ^ k)

── 3 · THE THREE SIZES ARE ONE DOUBLING, NOT THREE LITERALS ────────────────────────────────────────────── The key is two identifiers and the signature is two keys. Stated as the doubling so a reader sees the family rather than a coincidence of ratios among typed numbers.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 3 cases. □

Theorem 153 (the_doubling_survives_the_conversion)sealed.

toBits uuidBytes == 128 ∧ toBits publicKeyBytes == 256 ∧ toBits signatureBytes == 512 ∧ (List.range 3).all (fun k => toBits ([uuidBytes, publicKeyBytes, signatureBytes].getD k 0) == 128 * 2 ^ k)

── 4 · AND IN BITS THEY ARE THE SAME FAMILY ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── The conversion carries the structure, which is what makes the two files' vocabularies interchangeable rather than merely equal at one point.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 3 cases. □

Theorem 154 (the_key_and_the_signature_together_are_six_identifiers)sealed.

publicKeyBytes+signatureBytes=96∧96=6⋅uuidBytes∧toBits(publicKeyBytes+signatureBytes)=768∧768=6⋅container
publicKeyBytes + signatureBytes == 96 ∧ 96 == 6 * uuidBytes ∧ toBits (publicKeyBytes + signatureBytes) == 768 ∧ 768 == 6 * container
LaTeX source
\mathrm{publicKeyBytes} + \mathrm{signatureBytes} = 96 \land 96 = 6 \cdot \mathrm{uuidBytes} \land \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{publicKeyBytes} + \mathrm{signatureBytes}\right) = 768 \land 768 = 6 \cdot \mathrm{container}

── 5 · WHAT TRAVELS BESIDE THE IDENTIFIER, DECIDED IN BOTH UNITS ───────────────────────────────────────── asymmetric.lean states this in bytes only. A reader working in bits had to convert by hand, which is where a transposed figure enters.

Proof. by decide — by evaluation; no domain is walked. □

Theorem 155 (no_other_width_converts_to_this_container)sealed.

∀b∈{0,…,39},toBits(b)=container=b=uuidBytes∧toBits(15)≠container∧toBits(17)≠container
(List.range 40).all (fun b => (toBits b == container) == (b == uuidBytes)) ∧ toBits 15 != container ∧ toBits 17 != container
LaTeX source
\forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right) = \mathrm{container} = b = \mathrm{uuidBytes} \land \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(15\right) \neq \mathrm{container} \land \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(17\right) \neq \mathrm{container}

── 6 · THE CONTROL: THE BINDING IS NOT FREE ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Theorems 1 to 5 would all hold of a conversion that ignored its argument. A wrong width must fail, and the neighbouring widths do: no other byte count in range converts to this container.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 40 cases. □

Theorem 156 (the_constants_are_instances_of_the_law_and_not_the_law)sealed.

toBits(uuidBytes)=container∧∀b∈{0,…,39},toBits(b)=b⋅8∧∀b∈{0,…,39},b≠uuidBytes∨toBits(b)=container
toBits uuidBytes == container ∧ (List.range 40).all (fun b => toBits b == b * 8) ∧ (List.range 40).all (fun b => b != uuidBytes || toBits b == container)
LaTeX source
\mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{uuidBytes}\right) = \mathrm{container} \land \forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right) = b \cdot 8 \land \forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; b \neq \mathrm{uuidBytes} \lor \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right) = \mathrm{container}

── 7 · THE LEAD THIS CLOSES, NAMED ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Six of the forty-two "reads back a hand-set value" theorems are in asymmetric.lean over these three constants. They are not deleted and they are not wrong — what was missing is the law they are instances of, and it is here, decided over the domain rather than at the one point the constants sit at.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 1,600 cases. □

Theorem 157 (the_binding_is_consistency_and_not_derivation)sealed.

publicKeyBytes=32∧signatureBytes=64∧container=128∧∃b∈{0,…,59}:b≠publicKeyBytes∧toBits(b)=b⋅8
publicKeyBytes == 32 ∧ signatureBytes == 64 ∧ container == 128 ∧ (List.range 60).any (fun b => b != publicKeyBytes ∧ toBits b == b * 8)
LaTeX source
\mathrm{publicKeyBytes} = 32 \land \mathrm{signatureBytes} = 64 \land \mathrm{container} = 128 \land \exists b \in \{0,\dots,59\} : b \neq \mathrm{publicKeyBytes} \land \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right) = b \cdot 8

── 8 · WHOSE CONSTANTS THESE ARE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── NOT THIS DEPOSIT'S, AND NOTHING HERE MAKES THEM ITS OWN. 32 and 64 are Ed25519's, chosen by its designers for reasons of elliptic-curve security this file decides nothing about; 128 is RFC 9562's; eight bits to a byte is a convention older than both. What is decided is that this tree states them CONSISTENTLY across two files that previously could not contradict each other because nothing compared them. A binding is not a derivation: none of these widths follows from anything here, and if Ed25519 had chosen 48 bytes this file would bind 48 just as happily.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 60 cases. □

Theorem 158 (the_payload_and_the_check_exhaust_the_free_bits)sealed.

Asymmetric.payloadBits+Asymmetric.checkBits=Capacity.free∧Asymmetric.payloadBits+Asymmetric.checkBits=122∧Asymmetric.payloadBits+Asymmetric.checkBits+Capacity.reserved=Capacity.container
Asymmetric.payloadBits + Asymmetric.checkBits == Capacity.free ∧ Asymmetric.payloadBits + Asymmetric.checkBits == 122 ∧ Asymmetric.payloadBits + Asymmetric.checkBits + Capacity.reserved == Capacity.container
LaTeX source
\mathrm{Asymmetric.payloadBits} + \mathrm{Asymmetric.checkBits} = \mathrm{Capacity.free} \land \mathrm{Asymmetric.payloadBits} + \mathrm{Asymmetric.checkBits} = 122 \land \mathrm{Asymmetric.payloadBits} + \mathrm{Asymmetric.checkBits} + \mathrm{Capacity.reserved} = \mathrm{Capacity.container}

── 9 · THE PAYLOAD AND THE CHECK PARTITION THE FREE BITS ───────────────────────────────────────────────── A third file joins the binding, and this one was invisible until the widths were put side by side. asymmetric.lean types `checkBits = 32` and `payloadBits = 42 + 48`; imprint.lean and capacity.lean decide that a UUID has 122 free bits after the version and variant are reserved. 90 + 32 = 122 EXACTLY — the payload and its check do not merely fit in a UUID, they exhaust it, with nothing spare and nothing borrowed. Three files stating one layout, and nothing had ever compared them.

Proof. by decide — by evaluation; no domain is walked. □

Theorem 159 (the_check_is_exactly_what_the_payload_does_not_take)sealed.

∀pay∈{0,…,122},pay>Capacity.free∨pay+Capacity.free-pay=Capacity.free∧Capacity.free-Asymmetric.payloadBits=Asymmetric.checkBits∧Capacity.free-Asymmetric.checkBits=Asymmetric.payloadBits
(List.range 123).all (fun pay => pay > Capacity.free || pay + (Capacity.free - pay) == Capacity.free) ∧ Capacity.free - Asymmetric.payloadBits == Asymmetric.checkBits ∧ Capacity.free - Asymmetric.checkBits == Asymmetric.payloadBits
LaTeX source
\forall \mathrm{pay} \in \{0,\dots,122\},\; \mathrm{pay} > \mathrm{Capacity.free} \lor \mathrm{pay} + \mathrm{Capacity.free} - \mathrm{pay} = \mathrm{Capacity.free} \land \mathrm{Capacity.free} - \mathrm{Asymmetric.payloadBits} = \mathrm{Asymmetric.checkBits} \land \mathrm{Capacity.free} - \mathrm{Asymmetric.checkBits} = \mathrm{Asymmetric.payloadBits}

── 10 · AND NOTHING IS SPARE, WHICH IS A CHOICE AND NOT AN ACCIDENT ────────────────────────────────────── Stated as the law rather than at the point: for every split of the free bits, the check is what the payload does not take. The instance is one line of it, and the domain is decided so a future change to either constant cannot silently leave a gap or an overlap.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 123 cases. □

Theorem 160 (splitting_a_width_is_undone_by_either_part)sealed.

∀w∈{0,…,129},∀a∈{0,…,129},a>w∨w-a+a=w∧w-w-a=a
(List.range 130).all (fun w => (List.range 130).all (fun a => a > w || (w - a) + a == w && w - (w - a) == a))
LaTeX source
\forall w \in \{0,\dots,129\},\; \forall a \in \{0,\dots,129\},\; a > w \lor w - a + a = w \land w - w - a = a

A partition of a width is recovered by subtracting either part: the inverse of splitting is splitting.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 16,900 cases. □

Theorem 161 (reserving_and_freeing_are_inverse_at_every_width)sealed.

∀w∈{0,…,129},∀r∈{0,…,8},r>w∨w-r+r=w
(List.range 130).all (fun w => (List.range 9).all (fun r => r > w || (w - r) + r == w))
LaTeX source
\forall w \in \{0,\dots,129\},\; \forall r \in \{0,\dots,8\},\; r > w \lor w - r + r = w

Reserving bits and freeing them are inverse over every container, not only over 128.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 1,170 cases. □

Theorem 162 (one_more_bit_doubles_the_space_at_every_width)sealed.

∀b∈{0,…,16},2b+1=2⋅2b
(List.range 17).all (fun b => 2 ^ (b + 1) == 2 * 2 ^ b)
LaTeX source
\forall b \in \{0,\dots,16\},\; 2^{b + 1} = 2 \cdot 2^{b}

The capacity law: doubling the width squares the space, at every width in range.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 17 cases. □

Theorem 163 (the_space_recovers_the_width_it_came_from)sealed.

∀b∈{0,…,16},∀c∈{0,…,16},2b=2c=b=c
(List.range 17).all (fun b => (List.range 17).all (fun c => (2 ^ b == 2 ^ c) == (b == c)))
LaTeX source
\forall b \in \{0,\dots,16\},\; \forall c \in \{0,\dots,16\},\; 2^{b} = 2^{c} = b = c

And its inverse: the space determines the width back, for every exact power.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 289 cases. □

Theorem 164 (the_unit_conversion_is_a_bijection_on_multiples_of_eight)sealed.

∀b∈{0,…,39},toBytes(toBits(b))=b∧∀n∈{0,…,199},nmod8=0=toBits(toBytes(n))=n
(List.range 40).all (fun b => toBytes (toBits b) == b) ∧ (List.range 200).all (fun n => (n % 8 == 0) == (toBits (toBytes n) == n))
LaTeX source
\forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \mathrm{toBytes}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(b\right)\right) = b \land \forall n \in \{0,\dots,199\},\; n \bmod 8 = 0 = \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(\mathrm{toBytes}\mathopen{}\left(n\right)\right) = n

Bytes and bits, both directions, at every width — the law theorem 2 stated for one conversion.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 8,000 cases. □

Theorem 165 (the_laws_separate_widths_rather_than_collapsing_them)sealed.

∃w∈{0,…,11}:∃a∈{0,…,11}:a>w∧w-a=0∧a≠0∧27≠28∧toBits(5)≠toBits(6)
(List.range 12).any (fun w => (List.range 12).any (fun a => a > w && w - a == 0 && a != 0)) ∧ 2 ^ 7 != 2 ^ 8 ∧ toBits 5 != toBits 6
LaTeX source
\exists w \in \{0,\dots,11\} : \exists a \in \{0,\dots,11\} : a > w \land w - a = 0 \land a \neq 0 \land 2^{7} \neq 2^{8} \land \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(5\right) \neq \mathrm{toBits}\mathopen{}\left(6\right)

THE CONTROL ON ALL FIVE. Each is satisfied by arithmetic that ignores its arguments, so a case that must FAIL is required: subtracting more than a width does not return it, and unequal widths give unequal spaces. Without this the laws above are true of a tree where every width is the same width.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 144 cases. □

Theorem 166 (fitting_is_the_order_and_not_fitting_is_its_negation)sealed.

∀a∈{0,…,69},∀b∈{0,…,69},a≤b=¬b<a
(List.range 70).all (fun a => (List.range 70).all (fun b => (a ≤ b) == !(b < a)))
LaTeX source
\forall a \in \{0,\dots,69\},\; \forall b \in \{0,\dots,69\},\; a \le b = \lnot b < a

FITTING IS ≤, AND NOT FITTING IS ITS NEGATION. One law, both directions, at every pair of widths.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 4,900 cases. □

Theorem 167 (the_gap_is_what_is_left_and_adding_it_back_returns)sealed.

∀a∈{0,…,69},∀b∈{0,…,69},a>b∨b-a+a=b∧b-b-a=a
(List.range 70).all (fun a => (List.range 70).all (fun b => a > b || (b - a) + a == b && b - (b - a) == a))
LaTeX source
\forall a \in \{0,\dots,69\},\; \forall b \in \{0,\dots,69\},\; a > b \lor b - a + a = b \land b - b - a = a

THE GAP IS WHAT IS LEFT, AND ADDING IT BACK RETURNS THE LARGER — the inverse the lead asks for.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 4,900 cases. □

Theorem 168 (the_identifier_holds_neither_the_key_nor_the_signature)sealed.

uuidBytes<publicKeyBytes∧uuidBytes<signatureBytes∧publicKeyBytes-uuidBytes=uuidBytes∧signatureBytes-uuidBytes=3⋅uuidBytes
uuidBytes < publicKeyBytes ∧ uuidBytes < signatureBytes ∧ publicKeyBytes - uuidBytes == uuidBytes ∧ signatureBytes - uuidBytes == 3 * uuidBytes

THE INSTANCES, FOLLOWING FROM THE LAWS rather than typed beside them: the identifier holds neither.

Proof. by decide — by evaluation; no domain is walked. □

Theorem 169 (the_conversion_carries_the_containment)sealed.

∀a∈{0,…,69},∀b∈{0,…,69},a≤b=toBits(a)≤toBits(b)

And in bits, where the same order holds — the conversion carries the containment, not only the sizes.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 4,900 cases. □

Theorem 170 (the_gap_between_payload_and_check_is_their_difference)sealed.

∀pay∈{0,…,122},pay>Capacity.free∨pay≥Capacity.free-pay=pay+pay≥Capacity.free∧Asymmetric.payloadBits-Asymmetric.checkBits=58∧Asymmetric.checkBits+58=Asymmetric.payloadBits

THE PAYLOAD/CHECK GAP, as a law over every split of the free bits and then at its instance.

Theorem 171 (a_gap_in_bits_is_a_factor_in_space)sealed.

258⋅2Asymmetric.checkBits=2Asymmetric.payloadBits∧∀g∈{0,…,19},∀c∈{0,…,19},2g⋅2c=2g+c
2 ^ 58 * 2 ^ Asymmetric.checkBits == 2 ^ Asymmetric.payloadBits ∧ (List.range 20).all (fun g => (List.range 20).all (fun c => 2 ^ g * 2 ^ c == 2 ^ (g + c)))

AND THE GAP IN SPACE IS THE PRODUCT, not the difference — the error a reader makes reading bit gaps.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 400 cases. □

Theorem 172 (the_laws_separate_the_order_rather_than_collapsing_it)sealed.

¬publicKeyBytes≤uuidBytes∧¬signatureBytes≤publicKeyBytes-1∧signatureBytes-uuidBytes≠uuidBytes-signatureBytes∧uuidBytes-signatureBytes=0
!(publicKeyBytes ≤ uuidBytes) ∧ !(signatureBytes ≤ publicKeyBytes - 1) ∧ (signatureBytes - uuidBytes) != (uuidBytes - signatureBytes) ∧ uuidBytes - signatureBytes == 0
LaTeX source
\lnot \mathrm{publicKeyBytes} \le \mathrm{uuidBytes} \land \lnot \mathrm{signatureBytes} \le \mathrm{publicKeyBytes} - 1 \land \mathrm{signatureBytes} - \mathrm{uuidBytes} \neq \mathrm{uuidBytes} - \mathrm{signatureBytes} \land \mathrm{uuidBytes} - \mathrm{signatureBytes} = 0

THE CONTROL. Every law above is satisfied by arithmetic that ignores its arguments, so cases that must FAIL are required: a larger width does not fit in a smaller, and the gap is not symmetric.

Proof. by decide — by evaluation; no domain is walked. □

Theorem 173 (truncation_reports_no_gap_where_the_order_reverses)sealed.

∀a∈{0,…,39},∀b∈{0,…,39},a≤b=a-b=0∨a=b
(List.range 40).all (fun a => (List.range 40).all (fun b => (a ≤ b) == (a - b == 0) || a == b))
LaTeX source
\forall a \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; \forall b \in \{0,\dots,39\},\; a \le b = a - b = 0 \lor a = b

WHAT TRUNCATING SUBTRACTION HIDES, said rather than relied on. `a - b` is 0 when b exceeds a, so a gap read without its order is a gap that silently reports none — which is why every law above carries the comparison beside the subtraction.

Proof. by decide — exhausting 1,600 cases. □
