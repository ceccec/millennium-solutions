Textbooks ​

qpu seals each textbook example as an integer theorem whose docstring is its human sentence (Qpu/Textbook.lean). Each one is decoded here into a particle: the sentence, the statement the kernel checks, the variants that reflect it, and its coin — the content address of the key and statement. Every field is read off the two texts by npm run textbooks; nothing is judged by hand.

Read from qpu commit df0f26c6 — local to qpu’s tree, not yet on github.com/uuidna/qpu: 993 theorems, 391 base and the rest their variants (reflected 390, contrapositive 108, converse 53, refuted converse 51). 93 are physics, 8 of them re-derived independently from the operators in src/proof/quantum_textbooks.lean.

7 sentences state none of their statement's numbers — not a contradiction, a sentence that says the rule in words where the statement says it in numbers: prizes_computablebeacon_periodplugins_by_capabilitycompete_knowssitemap_capacitychat_call_or_questionstream_no_silence

The decoder's own control: a sentence sharing no numeral with its statement must decode as stating none of them, or the run fails.

Each coin holds its skills and tools. Its skills are the qpu doors that exercise it, read from qpu's own examples (45 skills); its tools are those example doors, this decoder, and the Lean theorem that re-derives it here when one does. Every door a coin names is resolved against qpu's examples: 4 do not — named in the qpu textbook table and defined nowhere in its code: jsonld-blockjsonld-cataloguecodes-planconfigure-cloudflare-adapters
